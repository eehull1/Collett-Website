Brokerage

Lease-up strategies designed to maximize the performance of real estate assets.

The Collett brokerage team serves clients in multiple ways: by providing market analysis, tenant representation, property listing and marketing services.

Whatever a client’s particular needs are, our brokers will find strategies and solutions that truly fit their needs. We approach everything with an open mind. Each situation is carefully assessed, and successful programs are then formulated. Our lease-up strategies are designed to maximize the performance of real estate assets.

Each Collett associate works in two ways: as a commercial leasing specialist within a specific category, and as a team member whose crossover skills bring strength to every project. Respected in the industry for their integrity and with a reputation for achieving results, our associates are proficient at maintaining high occupancy rates.

  • Investment sales
  • Land and outparcel sales
  • Property marketing
