Chicken Salad Chick to Make Greenville Debut

QSR Magazine – July 24, 2013

Chicken Salad Chick, the nation's newest, fast-casual restaurant concept, is scheduled to open South Carolina's first franchise on August 13, in Greenville. The restaurant is located in the Augusta Commons Shopping Center at 2222 Augusta Street, Unit #6, Greenville, South Carolina, 29605.

The Greenville owners, Julie Beville and Michelle Singleton of SingBev Hospitality LLC. have been loyal fans of the brand and wanted to bring it to Greenville following on the heels of successful franchise launches in Valdosta, Georgia, and Tallahassee, Florida, in early 2013.

“This extraordinary product is sure to be a huge hit with chicken salad lovers in Greenville,” owners Julie and Michelle say. “We look forward to opening more locations in the coming months and years through the Carolinas and North Georgia.” The Greenville location is the first of 29 Chicken Salad Chick locations to be opened by SingBev Hospitality LLC.
