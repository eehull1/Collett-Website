Management

Accessible, responsive management for the entire life cycle of a property.

We offer traditional property management services, maintaining retail properties and helping them operate efficiently. Owners and tenants can count on our accessibility, responsiveness and prompt personal service, as well as our attention to detail and experience.

Collett can also act as a trusted consultant that helps owners develop long-term investment strategies for their retail or mixed-use centers. Taking on the role of an asset or real estate manager, we can help a client determine the return on investment for updating or expanding a property, or what actions will improve its selling price. Through forecasting, budgeting and modeling, we can help a client maximize their return during the entire life cycle of a property.

What’s included

  • Day-to-day property operations
  • Lease administration and accounting
  • Asset strategy, budgeting and forecasting
  • Renovation and expansion analysis
