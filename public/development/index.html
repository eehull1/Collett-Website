Development

Projects that become the cornerstone of the communities they serve.

Over the last 30 years, Collett has played a significant role in shaping many Southeastern and South-Western communities through its passion for partnering with civic leaders, retailers and investors to address the needs of the community first. Whether we are developing in a small community or large urban cities, Collett specializes in balancing the needs of all stakeholders involved to create projects that become the cornerstone of each and every community we touch. As John Collett always preaches, “we are only as good as our last deal.” This mantra drives each member of the Development Team to focus on the small details that differentiate Collett from its peers in the industry.

Today Collett continues its long history of developing first class projects across a wide swath of the country. With key personnel located in Charlotte, NC, Dallas, TX, and Greenville, SC we are ideally suited to address the needs of our clients throughout the Southeast and Southwest. To date we have successfully completed the development of 18 million square feet of retail projects from small freestanding facilities to million square foot plus multi-tenant projects.

When development opportunities arise, we respond immediately and without hesitation to maximize financial returns whether for our own account or on behalf of our many valuable clients. We excel at identifying needs, developing partnerships, and coordinating multiple facets of the development business to come up with solutions that reward all the stakeholders involved.

What’s included

  • Site selection and market research
  • Entitlements and civic partnerships
  • Construction oversight
  • Lease-up and long-term positioning
