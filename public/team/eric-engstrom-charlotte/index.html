Eric Engstrom

Development Partner

  • 704.206.8337
  • Charlotte office

Eric Engstrom is a Development Partner at Collett focused on retail development throughout the Southeast. He joined Collett in 2023 to become part of the firm’s development team and has since worked closely with its partners to identify and execute retail development opportunities. His involvement spans site selection, acquisitions, tenant relationships, and coordination throughout the development process.

Eric brings a strong foundation in retail brokerage to his development work. Prior to joining Collett, he spent seven years with Whitney Commercial Real Estate in Asheville, representing owners, tenants, and buyers throughout western North Carolina. In addition to his development responsibilities, he continues to serve brokerage clients through seller and landlord representation in North Carolina and buyer and tenant representation across the Southeast.

Eric holds an undergraduate degree from North Carolina State University and an MBA from Wake Forest University School of Business.
