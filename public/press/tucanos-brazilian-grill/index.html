Tucanos Brazilian Grill

Source: Business Greenville Magazine

Tucanos Brazilian Grill, a fun and festive restaurant announced today that it will open its first-ever South Carolina location in Greenville on April 28, 2016. Located in the popular Magnolia Park Town Centre, Tucanos is a community-oriented, Brazilian-style churrascaria—a restaurant which combines the experience of traditional Brazilian grilling with the fun and flair of the beaches of Rio de Janeiro.

Expertly trained servers bring a parade of flame-grilled, seasoned meats, vegetables and fresh pineapple to the table, slicing sizzling portions off of skewers directly onto the guest’s plate. In addition to grilled items, Tucanos offers an award-winning Salad Festival, featuring 70 Brazilian and American specialties including fresh tropical fruits, gourmet salads, lobster bisque, stroganoff, feijoada (a traditional black bean stew), and new Brazilian features each month.

The restaurant’s interior features a mix of festive colors, decorative fabrics, custom granite countertops, artwork sourced from the streets of Brazil, and ornate waves created from colorful shards of tile. A custom bar and large windows create an open-air environment while multiple covered patios allow guests to take advantage Greenville’s subtropical temperatures. A private dining area fully equipped with audio/video capabilities is the perfect location for intimate celebrations or productive business engagements.

“Greenville is a wonderful, lively, family-friendly community, making it the perfect place to offer our authentic dining experience,” said Steve Oldham, Tucanos founder and president. “We’re excited that our first foray into the South is in the vibrant, thriving city of Greenville. We look forward to being a part of the wonderful things happening throughout the Upstate community.”

James Wagner, the general manager of the new Greenville Tucanos, added, “We couldn’t be more excited to announce our grand opening and give Greenville an escape from the ordinary. Our new team members are eager to serve our guests and bring the traditional Brazilian dining experience to the Upstate.”

Located at 1025 Woodruff Road, Tucanos’ operating hours are Monday – Thursday 11 a.m. – 10 p.m. and Friday and Saturday 11 a.m. – 11 p.m. Although reservations are not required, they are accepted by calling 864-288-2486. Tucanos will also offer catering services for holiday parties, weddings, luncheons and large events and can create a custom menu for any special occasion.

For more information about Tucanos Brazilian Grill, please visit http://www.tucanos.com/greenville or www.facebook.com/TucanosGreenville. To receive special offers, join Club Tucanos at www.tucanos.com/clubtucanos. Members earn one reward point for every dollar spent, as well as a free birthday meal during their birthday month.
